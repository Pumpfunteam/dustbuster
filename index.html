Why Do You Receive Free SOL?

Solana requires ~0.002039 SOL for every Token Account created. When you ape meme coins and dump them to 0, that rent remains frozen indefinitely. DustBuster executes closeAccount to return 100% of that native SOL deposit back to your wallet.

100% Non-Custodial Protocol

DustBuster only packages official Solana SPL Token instructions to close zero-balance accounts. We never request approvals, signatures for active balances, or transfer permissions for your funds.
